Common questions

A clearer start for your school.

What is ConnectEdu?

ConnectEdu is school management and parent engagement software for small schools in India. It brings communication, attendance, fees, transport and academics into one system.

How much does ConnectEdu cost?

Plans start at ₹8 per student per month. Book a demo to discuss the modules and rollout that fit your school.

Can it work with our existing biometric device?

ConnectEdu can integrate with a supported existing biometric device through a sync script on the connected school computer.

Is there an app for parents?

Yes. ConnectEdu has Android and iPhone apps where parents can see school notices, attendance, fees, report cards and transport updates.

How does the Admin AI Agent work?

Administrators ask a question in plain language. The agent finds supported school information, returns a clear answer and guides the administrator to the relevant record or next action.

Can the AI Agent add students and staff?

Yes. Give the Admin AI Agent the student’s or staff member’s name and required details, and it will add the record for the administrator.

Can parents pay fees through the mobile app?

Yes. Parents can pay through available payment gateways using methods such as UPI, credit card and net banking, then view the payment record in the app.

How does structured messaging work?

Every school-parent message has a clear purpose: announcement, acknowledgement, approval request, payment request, alert event or reply. Parents can immediately understand what the school needs them to read or do.

Is ConnectEdu only for large schools?

No. ConnectEdu is built for small and independent schools — simple to set up, with guided onboarding and video tutorials for staff.
