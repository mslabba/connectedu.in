Refund & Cancellation Policy

How refunds and cancellations work for school subscriptions and fee payments made through ConnectedU.

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 | Last Updated: September 26, 2026

Introduction

This Refund & Cancellation Policy is issued by InRay Labs Private Limited (CIN: U62099KL2026PTC104994) ("we", "us", "our") and applies to all payments made for the ConnectedU platform ("Platform"), including school subscription fees and parent fee payments collected through the Platform.

By making a payment on the Platform, you agree to the terms of this policy.

School Subscription Fees

  • School subscriptions are billed annually (or as per the agreed plan) and payable in advance.
  • Once a subscription period has commenced and the school's account is activated, the subscription fee for that period is non-refundable, except as required by law.
  • One-time onboarding, setup, migration, and training charges are non-refundable once the service has been rendered.
  • Schools may cancel their subscription at any time by writing to hello@inraylabs.com. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the already-paid period.
  • No pro-rata refunds are issued for partial use of a subscription period unless explicitly agreed in writing.

Parent Fee Payments Collected Through the Platform

  • Fee payments made by parents (via UPI, cards, or net-banking) are processed through our payment gateway partner and credited to the respective school.
  • InRay Labs Private Limited acts as the technology provider facilitating the transaction; the school is the merchant of record for fee dues.
  • Fee receipts are generated automatically on the Platform for every successful payment.

Failed, Duplicate & Erroneous Transactions

  • If an amount is debited from your account but the payment is not confirmed on the Platform, the transaction will be automatically refunded to the source within 5–7 business days. You do not need to raise a request for this.
  • Duplicate payments made for the same fee invoice will be refunded in full to the source.
  • If a payment is debited but not reflected even after 7 business days, please contact us with the transaction / UTR details for manual reconciliation.

How Refunds Are Processed

  • All approved refunds are credited only to the original source — the same UPI ID, card, or bank account used for the payment. Refunds cannot be issued in cash or to a different account.
  • Refunds are initiated within 2 business days of approval and typically reflect within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • GST, where applicable, is handled as per prevailing tax rules on the refunded amount.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, write to hello@inraylabs.com or call +91 9947702294 with:

  • Your name, school name, and registered phone number / email
  • Transaction ID / UTR number and date of payment
  • Amount paid and reason for the refund request

We will acknowledge your request within 2 business days and share a resolution timeline.
