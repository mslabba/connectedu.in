CEA Certificate From School: Format, Who Signs and How to Issue in Bulk

20 Nov 2026 · School Office · ConnectEdu Team

Book a Demo School Login

Every April, the school office gets the same request from dozens of parents at once: "Please give me the CEA certificate for last year." Parents who work for the Central Government need it to claim Children Education Allowance, and they need it signed by the head of the school. If the office types each one by hand, it eats a week. If the details are wrong, the parent's claim comes back and they come back to you.

This guide covers the CEA certificate from school: what it must confirm, a format you can adapt, when requests arrive, and how to issue them in bulk from the records you already keep.

What is a CEA certificate?

Children Education Allowance (CEA) is a fixed monthly amount that Central Government employees can claim for the schooling of up to two eldest children. Since 1 January 2024 it is ₹2,812.50 per month per child, and double (₹5,625) for a Divyang child, according to the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT) office memorandum dated 25 April 2024. Many public sector undertakings and autonomous bodies follow similar rules.

The amount is fixed. It does not depend on the fees the parent paid. That is why the employer does not ask for fee bills. It asks for proof that the child studied in your school during the year being claimed.

Under the DoPT consolidated instructions of 17 July 2018, that proof is a certificate issued by the Head of the Institution. The certificate should confirm that the child studied in the school during the previous academic year. If a parent cannot get the certificate, the same instructions let them submit a self-attested copy of the report card, or self-attested fee receipts (including e-receipts) showing the fee paid for the whole academic year.

What the certificate must confirm

The rule asks for one thing: that the child studied in your school for the academic year being claimed. In practice, the employer's office checks that the certificate matches the parent's claim form. So a useful CEA certificate carries:

  • The student's full name, as in your admission register
  • Admission or enrolment number
  • Date of birth
  • Father's or mother's name (the employee who is claiming)
  • Class and section studied in that year
  • The academic year, written in full (for example, April 2025 to March 2026)
  • The school's name, address, and affiliation number or school code
  • Date of issue, signature of the principal and the school seal

Some departments give employees their own proforma (CBSE, for example, attaches one to its own staff instructions). If a parent brings a department's format, fill that one. If not, use your standard format.

A CEA certificate format you can adapt

Print it on the school letterhead:

Certificate for Children Education Allowance

This is to certify that [student's full name], son/daughter of [parent's name], Admission No. [number], date of birth [DD-MM-YYYY], was a bona fide student of this school in Class [class and section] during the academic year [start month and year] to [end month and year].

[School name], [address]. Affiliation No. / School code: [number].

Date: [date of issue] Place: [city]

Signature of the Principal / Head of the Institution, with seal

Keep the wording plain. Do not add the fee amount unless the parent's department asks for it, since CEA does not depend on fees. Do not add marks or results either. The DoPT instructions say reimbursement has no link to the child's performance.

Hostel subsidy certificates are different

If you run a residential school or hostel, some parents will ask for a hostel subsidy certificate as well. Hostel subsidy is ₹8,437.50 per month (fixed) since 1 January 2024. It is admissible only when the residential school is at least 50 km from the employee's home.

For hostel subsidy, the 2017 DoPT memorandum asks for one more detail: the certificate should state the amount the parent spent on lodging and boarding in the residential complex. The employee is paid that amount or the ceiling, whichever is lower. So the hostel certificate needs figures from your fee or hostel ledger, not just the student record. Check them against what the parent actually paid.

When requests arrive, and how to handle them in bulk

The DoPT instructions say CEA is reimbursed once a year, after the financial year ends. That means requests pile up at the end of the academic year, from March to June, when parents file their claims. Some parents will also ask mid-year when they transfer.

A simple process that works:

  1. Know who will ask. At admission, note which parents work for the Central Government or a PSU. Many schools add a field to the admission form.
  2. Prepare a batch in March. Pull the list of those students with name, admission number, date of birth, parent, class and section for the year that is ending.
  3. Check the list once. A class teacher or office staff member checks the names and dates of birth against the admission register.
  4. Print, sign and seal in one sitting. The principal signs the batch instead of one certificate at a time.
  5. Hand them out with the report card, or send a note through the parent app saying when they can be collected.
  6. Keep an issue register. Note the certificate number, date of issue, student and who collected it. If a parent loses it, you can issue a duplicate marked "Duplicate".

Where each field comes from

Every field on a CEA certificate already sits in your student records. The work is pulling it out accurately.

Certificate field Where it lives
Name, date of birth, admission number Admission register / student record
Parent's name Student record (parent details)
Class and section for the year Promotion or class records for that academic year
Proof of the full year Attendance and report card for that year
Fees paid (only if asked) Fee ledger and receipts
Hostel boarding and lodging spend Hostel fee ledger

If these live in separate registers, the risk is a mismatch: a different spelling of the name, or a birth date that does not match the one on the employee's service records. If they live in a single student record, the office fills the certificate from one place.

Mistakes that send a claim back

  • The wrong academic year. The certificate must cover the year being claimed. Write the months, not just "2025-26".
  • Name spelling that differs from the claim form. Use the spelling in the admission register, and correct the register first if it is wrong.
  • Missing signature or seal. The rule names the Head of the Institution, so the principal signs, not a clerk.
  • Fee figures on a CEA certificate. They are not needed for CEA and only invite questions. Keep them for the hostel certificate.
  • A child who repeated a class after moving schools. The DoPT rules say CEA is not reimbursable if a child is admitted to the same class in another school after already passing it. Write the class correctly and let the employer decide.

What KVS-Samvaad shows about where this is going

On 12 November 2026, Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan launched KVS-Samvaad, a parent app and web portal (samvaad.kvs.gov.in). According to the Press Information Bureau release, parents can apply online for Bonafide and CEA certificates, along with student leave, without visiting the school.

Kendriya Vidyalayas were set up mainly for the children of Central Government employees, so these parents will soon expect the same from other schools: ask from a phone, collect without a trip to the office. A private school does not need a government portal for this. It needs accurate student records and a clear, fast process.

Frequently asked questions

Who signs the CEA certificate?

The Head of the Institution, normally the principal. The DoPT instructions name the head of the institution, so a certificate signed only by a clerk or class teacher may be returned.

Is a bonafide certificate the same as a CEA certificate?

They are close. Many schools use a bonafide certificate that confirms the child studied in the school during the academic year being claimed, and some departments call it that. The key is that it covers the full previous academic year and is signed by the head of the school.

What if the school cannot issue the certificate?

Under the DoPT instructions of 17 July 2018, the parent can submit a self-attested copy of the report card, or self-attested fee receipts (including e-receipts) showing the fee paid for the entire academic year.

How much is Children Education Allowance now?

₹2,812.50 per month per child (fixed), and ₹5,625 for a Divyang child, with effect from 1 January 2024, according to the DoPT memorandum of 25 April 2024. Hostel subsidy is ₹8,437.50 per month. These limits rise by 25% each time Dearness Allowance goes up by another 50%.

Should the certificate mention fees or marks?

Not for CEA. The amount is fixed and does not depend on fees or results. Hostel subsidy certificates are the exception: they should state what the parent spent on boarding and lodging.

When will parents ask for it?

Mostly from March to June, because CEA is reimbursed once a year after the financial year ends. Prepare a batch before the academic year closes.
