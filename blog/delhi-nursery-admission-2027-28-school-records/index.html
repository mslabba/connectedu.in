Delhi Nursery Admission 2027-28: The Records Your School Office Should Keep

19 Nov 2026 · Admissions · ConnectEdu Team

Book a Demo School Login

Every winter, the admission office of a Delhi private school runs the same race. The criteria have to be published, hundreds of forms come in over three weeks, every child gets a score, the lists go up, and then parents start asking why their child got 70 points and not 80. Most of the stress is not the decision. It is proving later that you followed your own rules.

This guide covers Delhi nursery admission 2027-28 from the school's side: what is different this year, the dates to plan around, and the records your office should keep at each step so the lists hold up when a parent, the Directorate of Education (DoE) or a court asks to see them.

What is different for 2027-28

The DoE's circular on the uniform age of admission and the restructured Foundational Stage (No. DE.23(749)/Sch.Br./2025, dated 24 October 2025) is now taking effect. It lays out three foundational classes before Class 1, with ages counted as on 31 March:

Class Age as on 31 March 2027
Nursery (Balvatika 1 / Pre-school 1) 3+ up to 4 years
Lower KG (Balvatika 2 / Pre-school 2) 4+ up to 5 years
Upper KG (Balvatika 3 / Pre-school 3) 5+ up to 6 years

For 2027-28 the circular says Lower KG and Upper KG are introduced as new classes. New admissions can be given in Lower KG to children who are 4+ as on 31 March 2027, and Upper KG takes only new admissions of children who are 5+. Last year's Nursery children move up to Lower KG, and last year's KG children move to Class 1 under the old structure.

In practice, your office may be handling entry at three levels this winter, not one. Watch the 2027-28 admission circular for whether the same points process applies to the new classes. The circular also lets the Head of School relax the minimum and maximum age by one month for Nursery to Class 1. Note every relaxation you grant, with the reason, in the child's file.

The dates to plan around

As of 19 November 2026, the DoE has not yet issued the 2027-28 nursery admission schedule. It usually comes in late November. Use last cycle's dates (2026-27, as reported from the DoE schedule) to plan staff and space, and swap in the new dates when the circular is out:

Step 2026-27 cycle
DoE schedule circular 22 November 2025
Schools upload criteria and points to the DoE module 28 November 2025
Forms open 4 December 2025
Last date for forms 27 December 2025
Upload details of children who applied (open seats) 9 January 2026
Upload points given to each child 16 January 2026
First list, with waiting list and points 23 January 2026
Parent queries on the first list 24 January to 3 February 2026
Second list 9 February 2026
Parent queries on the second list 10 to 16 February 2026
Closure of admissions 19 March 2026

Each of these steps leaves a record. If you plan the records now, the January uploads take an afternoon instead of a week.

Criteria and points: publish once, then freeze

Each private unaided school sets its own criteria and points for open seats, within the DoE's rules, and uploads them before forms open. Common criteria include neighbourhood distance, a sibling in the school and an alumni parent. Check the current circular for any criteria the DoE does not allow.

Before you upload:

  • Write each criterion with exactly how it is scored and which document proves it, for example "distance up to X km by the DoE method, proved by address proof".
  • Get the managing committee's sign-off and keep the signed copy with the date.
  • Put the same version on your school website and in the DoE module. Parents compare the two.
  • Save a PDF of what you uploaded. That version is the one every score will be checked against.

Do not change the criteria once forms open. If a correction is unavoidable, record what changed, why and when, and tell parents through the same channels you used to publish.

The application register

Every form needs a line in one register, whether it came in at the counter, by post or online:

  • form number, date and time received, and who received it;
  • the child's name, date of birth and age as on 31 March 2027;
  • the class applied for (Nursery, Lower KG or Upper KG);
  • parents' names, address and contact number;
  • the criteria the parent has claimed, and the documents attached;
  • the registration fee receipt number, if your school charges one within DoE limits;
  • any form returned or rejected, with the reason and the date the parent was told.

Give the parent an acknowledgement with the form number. Most "my form was lost" disputes end the moment you can show the register line and the acknowledgement.

A points sheet for every child

The score is where queries start. Keep a points sheet for each applicant that shows the working, not just the total:

Criterion Claimed by parent Document checked Points given Checked by
Neighbourhood Yes Address proof seen as per your published scale Staff name
Sibling in school Yes Sibling's admission number as per your published scale Staff name
Alumni parent No n/a 0 Staff name

Have a second person check a sample of sheets before the January upload, and keep the checked sheets with the forms. The total you upload to the DoE module should match the sheet exactly.

Lists, ties and the waiting list

When the first list goes up, it should show the points for each child, along with the waiting list. Keep:

  • the exact list you displayed, with the date, and a photo of the noticeboard;
  • the same list as published on the website, saved as a PDF;
  • how ties were broken. If your criteria use a draw of lots, record when it was held, who was present and the result, and keep any video;
  • each seat offered from the waiting list, in order, with the date the parent was told and whether they took it.

Answering parent queries on the points

The DoE schedule sets query windows after each list, and parents can raise a query in writing, by email or in person. Log every one in a query register: date, parent and form number, what they asked, who answered, the answer and the date. If a query leads to a change in points, update the points sheet, note the reason, and check whether the change moves anyone else on the list.

Answer from the points sheet, not from memory. A reply such as "your child received 20 of 25 neighbourhood points because the address proof places you in the second distance band" settles most queries. "The committee decided" does not.

EWS, DG and CWSN seats

Twenty-five per cent of entry-level seats in private unaided schools are reserved for children from economically weaker sections (EWS), disadvantaged groups (DG) and children with special needs (CWSN). These are filled through the DoE's separate centralised process, not your points system. Keep these records apart from the open-seat files:

  • the list of children allotted to your school by the DoE;
  • the date each family reported and the documents verified;
  • any admission refused, with the written reason and how the parent and DoE were told.

If your school claims reimbursement for these children later, the same file is your starting point. Our guide on RTE fee reimbursement for private schools covers what a claim pack needs.

After the lists: from form to student record

Once a child is admitted, the details your office collected on the form should become the child's student record, not be typed again for the fee counter, the class teacher and the transport desk. A single student record means one set of details for fees, attendance, transport and reports.

Frequently asked questions

When will Delhi nursery admission 2027-28 start?

The DoE has not issued the 2027-28 schedule as of 19 November 2026. Last cycle, the circular came on 22 November 2025, schools uploaded criteria on 28 November and forms opened on 4 December 2025. Expect a similar pattern and check edudel.nic.in for the circular.

What is the age for nursery admission in Delhi for 2027-28?

Under the DoE's Foundational Stage circular of 24 October 2025, Nursery is for children who are 3+ and up to 4 years as on 31 March 2027. Lower KG is 4+ up to 5, and Upper KG is 5+ up to 6.

What records should a school keep for nursery admission?

Keep the signed criteria and points as uploaded, an application register with every form, a points sheet for each child showing how the score was worked out, the lists as displayed with dates, the tie-break record, the waiting list movements and a register of parent queries with answers.

Can a school change its admission criteria after forms open?

It should not. Publish the criteria once, in the same version on the website and the DoE module. If a correction is unavoidable, record what changed and why, and tell parents through the same channels.

Are EWS and DG nursery seats filled through the school's points system?

No. The 25% EWS, DG and CWSN seats at entry level are filled through the DoE's separate centralised process. Keep those allotment and verification records separate from your open-seat files.
