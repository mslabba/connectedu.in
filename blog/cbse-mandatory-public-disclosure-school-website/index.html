CBSE Mandatory Public Disclosure: A School Website Checklist for Appendix IX

11 Nov 2026 · Compliance · ConnectEdu Team

Book a Demo School Login

Every CBSE-affiliated school has to keep a Mandatory Public Disclosure page on its own website, in the format CBSE prescribes in Appendix IX of the Affiliation Bye-Laws. It is the one compliance item that nobody logs in to check, so it quietly goes stale: a fire certificate expires, a teacher leaves, last year's fee structure stays up.

November is a good month to fix it. Admission enquiries for the next session usually start around now, and parents shortlisting schools open the website first. As Saral Techies pointed out (3 November 2026), what is on the website should match what the school has submitted to CBSE, and it needs updating every year. This post walks through Appendix IX section by section and gives the school office a yearly routine to keep the page current. It is written for principals, office staff and trustees of CBSE schools.

What Appendix IX asks for

CBSE publishes the blank proforma on its affiliation portal: Appendix IX, Mandatory Public Disclosure (PDF on saras.cbse.gov.in). It has five lettered sections, and the page on your website should follow the same order so that anyone checking it can read straight down.

Section What it covers Rows
A. General information Who the school is Name, affiliation number, school code, full address with PIN, principal's name and qualification, school email, phone
B. Documents and information Certificates, uploaded as copies Affiliation letter, trust/society/company registration, state NOC, RTE recognition, building safety, fire safety, DEO certificate or self-certification, water, health and sanitation certificates
C. Result and academics Fees, calendar, committees, results Fee structure, annual academic calendar, SMC list, PTA list, last three years' Class X and XII board results
D. Staff (teaching) Who teaches Principal, total teachers split into PGT, TGT and PRT, teacher-section ratio, special educator, counsellor and wellness teacher
E. School infrastructure The campus Campus area, number and size of classrooms and labs, internet facility, girls' and boys' toilets, link to a YouTube video of the school's infrastructure

Read the PDF itself before you change your page. The format is CBSE's; this table is only a summary of it.

Section A: get the basics exactly right

This looks like the easy part, and it is where small mismatches creep in.

  • Use the school name exactly as it appears on the affiliation letter, including the trust's spelling.
  • Give both the affiliation number and the school code; they are different numbers.
  • Update the principal's name and qualification the week a new principal joins, not at the next website refresh.
  • Use an official school email and a phone number that someone actually answers during admission season.

Section B: the eight documents, and the four that expire

Every row in Section B is an upload. The proforma carries a note that the copies must be self-attested by the Chairman, Manager or Secretary and by the Principal, and that if an uploaded document is later found not to be genuine, the school is liable for action as per norms.

Some of these documents never change, like the trust registration. Others carry validity dates and need renewal: building safety, fire safety, water, health and sanitation certificates, and in many states the RTE recognition too. A page that was correct when it went up becomes wrong on its own the day one of these lapses.

What to do:

  1. Make a one-page register of all eight documents: name, issuing authority, issue date, valid-till date, and who in the office owns the renewal.
  2. Scan each one properly as a PDF. A tilted phone photo of a certificate is hard to read and looks careless.
  3. Replace the file on the website the same week a renewal arrives, and keep the old copy in the office file.
  4. If your state also asks for a recognition file, our private school recognition document pack covers the overlapping papers, and the school maintenance and safety inspection checklist helps before a fire or building inspection.

Section C: fees, calendar, committees and results

Fee structure. Publish the fee structure for the current session, by class, with every fee head the school actually charges. It should match what the accounts office bills and what appears on receipts. A fee page that still shows last year's numbers is the fastest way to lose a parent's trust in admission season. Several states also have their own fee display rules, so check those alongside CBSE's.

Annual academic calendar. Put up the current session's calendar, not the one from two years ago, and replace it when the next session's calendar is approved.

SMC and PTA lists. Update the School Management Committee and Parent Teacher Association lists when members change, usually after the PTA general body meeting.

Board results for three years. The proforma asks for separate Class X and Class XII tables with the year, the number of registered students, the number who passed, the pass percentage and remarks. Use registered students as CBSE counts them, taken from your LOC records, so your pass percentage matches the Board's own figures. If you are tidying LOC records anyway, see our CBSE LOC checklist for the school office.

Section D: the staff rows that change every year

Section D asks for the principal, the total number of teachers split into PGT, TGT and PRT, the teacher-section ratio, and details of the special educator and the counsellor and wellness teacher.

Three things to watch:

  • The teacher-section ratio is a calculation, not a headcount. Divide teachers by the number of sections you are actually running this session. If sections were added in June, the old ratio is wrong.
  • Staff turnover makes this section go stale fastest. CBSE has repeatedly reminded schools to keep teacher details on their websites updated; EdunodeX notes a reminder circular dated 12 January 2026. Check the list every time someone joins or leaves, not once a year.
  • Fill every row. If the special educator or counsellor row is empty, it is visible to every parent and inspector who opens the page.

Section E: infrastructure and the video link

Section E asks for campus area in square metres, the number and size of classrooms and labs (including computer labs), whether the school has internet, the number of girls' and boys' toilets, and a link to a YouTube video covering the school's infrastructure.

The video row surprises many schools. Record a simple walk-through of classrooms, labs, library, toilets and play area, upload it, and link it. Re-shoot it when a new block or lab opens, so the numbers in this section and the video say the same thing.

A yearly routine for the school office

Give the page one owner in the office and put these dates in the calendar:

When What to update
Start of session (April–June) Fee structure, academic calendar, section count and teacher-section ratio, staff list
After board results (May–July) Add the new year's Class X and XII results; drop the oldest year
After the PTA general body meeting SMC and PTA member lists
Whenever a certificate is renewed Replace that document in Section B
Whenever a teacher joins or leaves PGT/TGT/PRT counts and staff details
October–November, before admissions Full read-through against the Appendix IX PDF, on a phone

And keep the Mandatory Public Disclosure link in the website's main menu or footer, where a parent or an inspector can find it in one tap.

Common mistakes

  • A "disclosure" page that is really an About Us page: history, vision, a fee table, and none of the Appendix IX rows.
  • Certificates that have expired but are still on the page.
  • Pass percentage worked out from students who appeared, not students registered.
  • A staff list that still names teachers who left last year.
  • Fee structure on the website that does not match what the accounts office charges.
  • Image files instead of readable PDFs, or links that open to a "file not found" page.

Frequently asked questions

What is CBSE Mandatory Public Disclosure?

It is the information every CBSE-affiliated school must publish on its own website in the format given in Appendix IX of the CBSE Affiliation Bye-Laws: general information, certificates, fee structure and results, teaching staff, and infrastructure.

Where can I find the Appendix IX format?

CBSE publishes the blank proforma as a PDF on its affiliation portal, saras.cbse.gov.in. Build your page in the same section order.

Who must attest the documents uploaded on the school website?

The proforma says the copies must be self-attested by the Chairman, Manager or Secretary and by the Principal.

How often should the disclosure page be updated?

At least once a year at the start of the session, and also whenever a certificate is renewed, results are declared, committee members change, or a teacher joins or leaves.

Should the results table use students who appeared or students registered?

The proforma asks for the number of registered students, the number passed and the pass percentage, so use registered students as per your LOC records.

Is this official CBSE guidance?

No. It is a planning summary of CBSE's Appendix IX format for school offices. Always check the latest format and circulars on CBSE's own websites before updating your page.
